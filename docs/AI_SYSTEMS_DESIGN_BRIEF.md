# AI Systems Showcase - Design Brief

## Design read

Reading this as a hidden B2B case-study landing page for operators, with a calm hospitality-aware systems language leaning toward editorial operations software rather than a SaaS dashboard. The page must make an interconnected business legible, credible, and specific to Rent With Heldy. Density can rise inside diagrams, but the reading path stays spacious and human. Quality means the page remains persuasive when every advanced animation is removed.

## Skill loadout and dials

- Surface: **marketing / landing / portfolio**
- Sole opinion skill: `taste-skill`
- Motion character: `emil-design-skills`
- Motion entry point: `motion-intelligence` through `motion-director`
- `DESIGN_VARIANCE: 7` - controlled asymmetry, varied section compositions, strict single-column mobile collapse
- `MOTION_INTENSITY: 4` - restrained explanatory reveals and quick state feedback only
- `VISUAL_DENSITY: 5` - technical enough to show systems, never cockpit-like

## System and brand overrides

- Extend the existing warm white, sand, ink navy, muted teal, Bricolage Grotesque, and Inter tokens. This overrides taste-skill's default Inter avoidance.
- Keep one light theme. Do not activate dormant dark tokens or add a theme toggle solely for this page.
- No generated imagery or stock photography. The feature specification requires system maps, workflow diagrams, and replaceable artifact structures; the brand also prohibits AI imagery. Pass 3 will supply sanitized real artifacts.
- The explicit hero CTA may function as section navigation, despite taste-skill's default scroll-cue prohibition.
- The hero and final business-contact actions retain their specified labels because each has a distinct narrative job.
- Cards are reserved for interactive systems or artifact containers. Editorial rules, connectors, type scale, and whitespace carry the rest.

## Acceptance criteria

1. All ten specified sections tell one continuous story and feel unmistakably connected to Rent With Heldy.
2. The route is hidden from navigation and sitemap, resolves directly, and emits `noindex,nofollow` without changing other routes.
3. Desktop diagrams become deliberate vertical or horizontally scrollable reading patterns on mobile, with no page-level overflow at 320px.
4. Opportunity Finder is deterministic, keyboard/touch accessible, and exposes its state in text rather than color alone.
5. The rendered surface earns an Anti-Slop verdict of **DISTINCTIVE** and passes Accessibility, Motion, and Mobile UX reviews with no blockers.
6. Motion uses the lowest valid engine tier, survives reduced motion, and is browser verified against its spec.
7. Existing booking, forms, fleet data, admin, shared navigation, and indexed SEO behavior remain untouched.

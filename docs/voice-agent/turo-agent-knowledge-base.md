## 2. Fleet guidance

The fleet spans practical economy cars and sedans, compact and midsize SUVs, larger SUVs, and newer premium vehicles. Never promise a specific vehicle, rate, seat count, fuel type, transmission, feature, mileage allowance, pet status, or delivery option without checking the live listing for the requested dates.

Use these discovery questions:

1. Pickup and return date/time?
2. Pickup location?
3. Number of adults and children?
4. Number and size of bags, strollers, wheelchairs, beach items, or other cargo?
5. Any car-seat, accessibility, premium, fuel-economy, or feature needs?

## 3. Booking and eligibility

- In the U.S., Turo's age requirement depends on the vehicle class and assessed value. Standard vehicles may be available from age 18; higher-value, Deluxe, Super Deluxe, Classic, or Specialty vehicles have higher minimums. Never state that every Rent With Heldy vehicle has the same age minimum.
- The booking person is the primary driver. They must complete Turo's eligibility and verification process and must pick up and return the vehicle.
- At check-in, the primary driver needs the current, valid physical license used for verification. Digital licenses, screenshots, photocopies, learner's permits, expired licenses, and other ineligible forms are not accepted.
- Guests may not book a Turo trip for someone else.
- Checkout price may include vehicle rate, trip fee, delivery, protection, young-driver fee, taxes, airport fee, parking, and Extras. Only the current Turo checkout screen can give the final total.
- Account, identity verification, payment, protection selection, cancellation, and booking errors are Turo-controlled. Rent With Heldy can explain and assist but cannot bypass the platform.

Official source: [Booking a car in the US](https://help.turo.com/en_us/booking-a-car-in-the-us-H1gxVVeE9)

## 4. Additional drivers

- A primary guest can request additional drivers through Turo. They must create their own Turo account, meet age/license requirements, and be approved.
- Hosts cannot approve a driver by phone, verbally, or through an informal message.
- Only Turo-approved drivers may drive. Allowing an unapproved driver can void the guest's protection.
- The primary guest remains financially responsible and must personally pick up and return the vehicle.
- Neither the host nor Turo can switch the primary driver on an existing trip. The original trip generally must be canceled and rebooked by the intended primary driver, subject to Turo's cancellation terms.

Guest instruction: Open Trips → Manage trip → Add a driver, then follow the prompts.

Official source: [Adding a driver to a trip](https://help.turo.com/adding-a-driver-to-a-trip-HksENgV5)

## 5. Check-in

- Turo check-in can begin up to 24 hours before scheduled start.
- The primary guest completes identity/license steps in the secure Turo flow.
- For a U.S. trip, required pre-trip verification tasks should be completed no later than the deadline shown by Turo, commonly two hours before trip start. Missing the deadline can cause cancellation. The live checklist controls.
- For in-person pickup, the guest presents the same current, valid physical license.
- Before driving, guest and host should document exterior, interior, odometer, fuel/charge level, and existing condition with clear, time-stamped Trip photos.
- Guest should confirm legal/safe condition, cleanliness, tires, visible damage, and dashboard warnings.
- If unsafe or materially unsatisfactory, the guest should not complete pickup or drive; they should contact Rent With Heldy and Turo support immediately.
- An early start of up to one hour may be allowed only if host and vehicle are ready, both parties agree, and full Turo check-in is completed.

Official source: [Trip check-in guide — Guests](https://help.turo.com/en_us/trip-check-in-guide-guests-HkZfVVe49)

## 7. Flight delays and location/time changes

- Ask guest to share the flight number and notify Rent With Heldy early through Turo messaging.
- A message helps coordination but does not change the official trip time or location.
- For a new time or location, guest must submit the appropriate Change trip request in Turo. The change is valid only after it appears as approved/updated in the trip details.
- Never promise that a vehicle can wait indefinitely, that a delivery charge will be waived, or that a delayed start automatically produces a refund.

## 8. Extensions, early returns, and late returns

- Extensions must be requested through Turo: booked trip → Change trip. Turo may approve automatically or route to host review.
- Verbal or written host agreement outside the formal Turo change is not a valid extension.
- Never say approved while the request is pending.
- Guest should return at scheduled end time. Turo currently provides a 30-minute grace period before a host may charge additional usage.
- If more than 30 minutes late, guest should request an extension immediately and notify host.
- Returning less than 3 hours early may be coordinated in Turo messaging with host permission. More than 3 hours early requires a formal Change trip request. Early return does not automatically mean refund.

Official sources: [Returning a vehicle and checking out](https://help.turo.com/en_us/returning-a-vehicle-and-checking-out-SymM4ExE9) and [Requests to extend or shorten a trip](https://help.turo.com/en_us/managing-requests-to-extend-or-shorten-a-trip-BJDjrVg45)

## 9. Checkout and return

Guest checklist:

1. Confirm current return location and key instructions.
2. Return at booked time.
3. Restore fuel/charge to required level.
4. Remove belongings and trash.
5. Photograph exterior, interior, odometer, and fuel/charge gauge in Turo checkout.
6. Upload photos before leaving.
7. For garage/lot returns, message exact level, zone, section, and space plus a location photo.
8. Leave ticket and key exactly as instructed.
9. Lock the vehicle and confirm checkout is complete.

Guest is responsible for legal/safe street parking and may be responsible for tickets/towing arising within the period defined by Turo after trip end.

Official source: [Returning a vehicle and checking out](https://help.turo.com/en_us/returning-a-vehicle-and-checking-out-SymM4ExE9)

## 10. Fuel and EV charging

- Return gas/diesel vehicle with same fuel level as check-in and use the vehicle's specified fuel grade.
- Return EV or plug-in hybrid at the required charge level. Live listing/trip instructions control.
- Guests should save fuel receipts and upload receipt plus gauge photos.
- Host may invoice eligible missing-fuel/recharging costs through Turo after trip.
- Tesla Supercharger and eligible non-Tesla charging, idle, or congestion fees tied to the trip may be invoiced.
- Do not quote exact reimbursement or low-battery fees unless the current Turo page/tool is checked during the call.

Official source: [Paying for fuel or EV charging](https://help.turo.com/en_us/paying-for-fuel-rJkqN4eV9)

## 11. Mileage

- Included distance and additional-distance rate appear in the listing, checkout, confirmation, trip details, and check-in.
- Limits vary by vehicle and trip length. Some listings include unlimited mileage or offer it as an Extra.
- Unlimited mileage Extra can only be added if offered and before trip start.

- Never estimate overage without live trip details.

Official source: [Driving distance included in a trip](https://help.turo.com/en_us/distance-included-in-trip-ryIbENeV9)

## 12. Tolls, tickets, parking, and post-trip invoices

- Tolls incurred during trip may be billed afterward through Turo. Rent With Heldy policy is intended to pass through actual tolls without its own added markup; owner must confirm before launch.
- Guest should not remove, move, cover, or replace a toll transponder.
- Host may submit supported toll costs through Turo up to Turo's current deadline.
- Eligible tickets, parking violations, towing/impound costs, fuel, charging, extra distance, and policy violations may create post-trip invoices.
- Guest generally has 48 hours to pay or dispute a post-trip invoice in Turo.
- Agent must not collect invoice payment directly, decide a dispute, or threaten collection.

Official sources: [Charging a guest for tolls](https://help.turo.com/en_us/charging-for-tolls-and-london-based-costs-SkCOrNlE9) and [Paying an invoice or unpaid balance](https://help.turo.com/en_us/paying-an-invoice-or-unpaid-balance-ryLNV4g4c)

## 13. Smoking and cleaning

- Smoking and vaping are prohibited in every Turo vehicle, including tobacco, marijuana, substitutes, e-cigarettes, and personal vaporizers.
- Turo requires physical evidence for a host smoking violation report; odor alone is not enough under the current host policy.
- Guests are not expected to clean ordinary trip mess before return.
- A cleaning violation is for severe or abusive condition such as bodily fluids, major stains/residue requiring professional work, extreme caked mud/sand/insects, or significant pet hair.
- Ordinary trash, crumbs, light sand/dirt, removable exterior dirt, minor spills, or odor alone generally do not qualify for a cleaning violation.
- Agent should state the policy, document the concern, and transfer disputes. Never promise or threaten a fee.

Official sources: [No smoking policy — Guests](https://help.turo.com/en_us/no-smoking-policy-or-guests-SyW04NgN9) and [Cleaning policy — Guests](https://help.turo.com/en_us/cleaning-and-disinfection-policy-or-guests-rkLCVVeVq)

## 14. Pets and service animals

- Service and assistance animals as defined by applicable law are permitted. Do not challenge, diagnose, or demand proof beyond approved legal/business workflow.
- Emotional-support or comfort-only animals do not automatically qualify as service animals under Turo policy.
- Non-service pets require a pet-friendly vehicle or host's written permission in Turo messaging.
- Turo generally requires non-service pets to be in a crate/carrier.
- Significant animal hair or an unauthorized non-service pet may be handled through Turo policy; transfer any dispute.

Official source: [Service animal and pet policy — Guests](https://help.turo.com/service-animal-and-pet-policy-or-guests-H1Y0N4eVq)

## 15. Cancellations and no-shows

- Guest should cancel through Trips → Manage trip → Change trip → Cancel trip.
- Refund eligibility and guest cancellation fees depend on whether the trip is refundable, booking time, cancellation time, trip length, and current Turo policy. Check the live cancellation screen; never promise an amount by phone.
- Under current U.S. guidance, a refundable trip generally has free cancellation through 24 hours before start, with a limited one-hour window after booking when booked inside 24 hours. Non-refundable trips have different rules. Live terms control.
- The host should never ask a guest to cancel on the host's behalf.
- A U.S. guest may be reported as a no-show for failing to appear within the current Turo window, sending someone else to pick up, failing to present a valid license, or failing required license confirmation.
- Flight delay, flight cancellation, lost baggage, and certain extenuating circumstances may receive special review. Guest should message host immediately and follow Turo's current process.
- Agent must not label the guest a no-show, promise a refund, or threaten a fee. Transfer the case to a human/Turo.

Official sources: [Canceling a trip with your host](https://help.turo.com/en_us/canceling-a-trip-with-your-host-HJyUE4xN9) and [Failing to show up for a trip](https://help.turo.com/failing-to-show-up-for-a-trip-HJUfENeVq)

## 16. Travel area and borders

- A vehicle booked in the U.S. may generally be driven across U.S. state lines. A host cannot create an unsupported state-line restriction.
- Turo currently permits a U.S.-booked vehicle to cross the U.S.–Canada border, subject to current documentation and trip rules.
- A U.S.-booked Turo vehicle may not cross the U.S.–Mexico border.
- Ask before any international border crossing. Confirm the registration, insurance card, car-sharing agreement, mileage, trip length, roadside implications, and vehicle-specific condition.
- A long trip to the Keys, Orlando, another state, or Canada does not change the included mileage. Check the live distance allowance.

Official source: [Driving across borders](https://help.turo.com/driving-across-borders-r1rjLfrgi)

## 17. Prohibited and unsafe use

Never encourage or approve:

- unapproved drivers;
- racing, speed contests, driver training, or reckless driving;
- driving under the influence;
- illegal activity;
- unauthorized towing/pushing;

## 18. Breakdown, flat tire, lockout, dead battery, or warning light

Immediate script logic:

1. Ask whether everyone is safe.
2. If injury, fire, fuel odor, threatening situation, smoke, or traffic danger: call 911.
3. If safe: stop driving when there is red warning light, steering/brake problem, severe vibration, flat tire, leak, smoke, overheating, or damage affecting safety.
4. Turn on hazards and move safely only if doing so will not worsen danger/damage.
5. Contact Rent With Heldy.
6. Call Turo U.S. 24/7 roadside: +1 (415) 965-4525.
7. Do not authorize own tow or repairs without Turo/host direction.

Turo U.S. customer support for help rebooking after an eligible roadside event is currently +1 (415) 963-4109. Live Turo instructions override.

Official source: [Roadside assistance — US guests](https://help.turo.com/en_us/roadside-assistance-or-us-guests-BJBiEVgE9)

## 19. Accident or damage

If immediate danger/injury: call 911.

If safe:

- contact Rent With Heldy;
- do not admit fault or argue;
- exchange other driver's full name, address, contact, registration, and insurer information;
- photograph all vehicles, damage, plates, roadway, and surroundings;
- collect witness contact information;
- provide the Turo incident information card;
- report through Turo.

If undriveable: call Turo roadside +1 (415) 965-4525. If still safely driveable, current Turo guidance says contact host and report the damage within 24 hours of incident.

Agent cannot decide fault, claim eligibility, coverage, out-of-pocket amount, reimbursement, repair method, or whether guest should settle directly. Transfer to Turo/claims/human.

Official sources: [Reporting damage — Guests](https://help.turo.com/en_us/reporting-damage-or-guests-SJtWr4gE9) and [Roadside assistance — US guests](https://help.turo.com/en_us/roadside-assistance-or-us-guests-BJBiEVgE9)

## 24. Approved short answers

“Can my spouse drive?”

Yes, once Turo approves them as an additional driver. Add them from Manage trip; the primary driver still needs to pick up and return the car.

“Can I pick up curbside at MIA?”

No. MIA currently prohibits Turo curbside handoffs. Your trip details will show the approved garage or Economy Park & Ride location.

“Can I pick up curbside at FLL?”

No. FLL currently prohibits Turo curbside handoffs. The primary approved area is Cypress Garage, Level 7, Zone 7NN; follow the live trip instructions.

“My flight is late. Am I okay?”

Message us through Turo with the new arrival time. If the official trip start needs to move, submit a Change trip request; a message alone does not change the reservation.

“Can I keep the car another day?”

Please open the trip and submit Change trip. It is approved only when Turo shows the new end time in your trip details.

“Do I have to wash the car?”

No professional cleaning is expected for ordinary use. Remove your belongings and trash, and return it without severe or abusive mess.

“Can I pay you with Zelle?”

Not for a Turo trip. Trip charges and post-trip invoices must stay in Turo.

“How much is the car?”

The rate changes by vehicle, dates, delivery, and other trip choices. Give me the dates and pickup location, and I’ll check the live total.

“I got a flat tire.”

Are you in a safe location? If yes, stop driving, turn on hazards, contact us, and call Turo U.S. roadside at +1 (415) 965-4525. If you are in danger or blocking traffic, call 911 first.

“Will I be charged for this damage?”

I can document and route the issue, but I can’t decide fault, coverage, or charges. Turo and the claims process will review the evidence and trip terms.

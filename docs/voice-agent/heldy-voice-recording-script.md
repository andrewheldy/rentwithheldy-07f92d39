# Heldy Voice Recording Script

Target length: about 30–40 minutes at a relaxed conversational pace.

Read the spoken words only. The section headings and bracketed notes are directions, not dialogue. Sip water between sections. Keep your distance from the microphone consistent.

## 1. Natural introduction — warm and grounded

[Read as if one new guest has just called you. Friendly, relaxed, and unhurried.]

Hi, this is Heldy with Rent With Heldy. Thanks for calling. How can I help make your trip a little easier today?

We’re a local, family-owned car rental team serving Fort Lauderdale, Miami, and the surrounding South Florida area. We help travelers, families, local residents, cruise passengers, and people who simply need a dependable vehicle for a few days.

What matters most to us is keeping the process clear. You should know which vehicle you’re booking, where you’re picking it up, what you need to bring, and who to contact if something changes. Travel can already be stressful. Getting the car should be the easy part.

If you tell me your dates, the number of passengers, how much luggage you’ll have, and where you want to pick up, I can help narrow down the best fit. If you’re flying, your airline and flight number are also helpful. If you’re cruising, tell me the ship, port, and expected arrival time.

We serve travelers arriving at Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport, which most people call F-L-L, and Miami International Airport, or M-I-A. We also coordinate pickups near Port Everglades, PortMiami, hotels, vacation rentals, body shops, and other approved locations across Broward and Miami-Dade.

## 2. Vehicle guidance — practical and conversational

[Sound helpful, not salesy.]

Let’s find a vehicle that actually fits your trip.

For one or two people traveling light, an economy car or sedan is usually the simplest choice. It’s easy to park, comfortable around town, and generally more fuel efficient.

For a family with luggage, beach gear, a stroller, or car seats, I usually recommend an S-U-V. A compact S-U-V may work for a smaller family, while a midsize or full-size S-U-V gives everyone more room.

For a larger group, passenger count is only half the question. Luggage takes up space quickly. Eight passengers and eight full-size suitcases may need much more room than the seating number suggests. I would rather help you choose honestly than put you in something that feels cramped at pickup.

If you want a newer premium vehicle, tell me what matters most: comfort, luggage room, performance, or a particular feature. We can look at the current live options. Availability and pricing change by date, so I’ll never guess. I’ll confirm the actual listing before I promise a specific vehicle or rate.

Some names and features you may hear in our fleet include Volkswagen Jetta, Volkswagen Taos, Toyota Corolla, Honda Accord, Honda Fit, Honda C-R-V, Subaru Forester, Ford Edge, Chevrolet Equinox, Chevrolet Suburban, Jeep Renegade, Kia Soul, Audi A-four, Audi Q-five, and Mercedes-Benz E-three-fifty.

Features vary from vehicle to vehicle. They may include Bluetooth, Apple CarPlay, a backup camera, all-wheel drive, third-row seating, premium sound, or advanced driver-assistance features. I’ll check the listing before confirming any particular feature.

## 3. Booking explanation — simple and precise

[Steady, matter-of-fact delivery.]

To get started, I’ll need your pickup date and time, return date and time, preferred pickup location, passenger count, luggage needs, and a good mobile number. I can answer questions and help you choose, but the reservation, payment, identity verification, protection selection, and final trip changes must be completed through the official booking platform.

For a Turo trip, the person who books must be the primary driver. The primary driver must meet Turo’s eligibility requirements and present a current, valid physical driver’s license. A screenshot, photocopy, learner’s permit, or digital license is not a substitute for the physical license at pickup.

If someone else may drive, they need to be added and approved through Turo as an additional driver. The primary driver is still responsible for the trip and must pick up and return the vehicle. A host cannot approve an additional driver by phone or text.

Please do not send a full driver’s-license image, payment card number, Social Security number, or account password over the phone. Use the secure steps in the Turo app. If the app asks you to upload identification, follow the instructions there.

Prices can change with dates, trip length, demand, delivery location, protection choices, taxes, airport fees, and optional extras. I can explain the pieces, but the checkout screen is the final source for the total price.

## 4. Airport pickup — reassuring and clear

[Sound like a calm local concierge.]

Flying into South Florida? Share your flight number after booking so we can plan around your arrival and send the exact pickup instructions before you land.

At Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport, Turo’s approved pickup and return area is generally the Cypress Garage, Level Seven, Zone Seven N-N. Reserved Turo spaces may be available there. If those spaces are full, follow the current instructions in your booked trip and our Turo messages. Curbside pickup is not allowed on airport property.

At Miami International Airport, approved locations include the Economy Park and Ride Lot, Dolphin Garage, Flamingo Garage, and Ibis Garage. Curbside handoffs are not permitted anywhere on M-I-A airport property. Always use the current location shown in the app and the instructions we send through Turo.

Airport permits, parking rules, and parking charges are controlled by the airport and Turo and can change. The airport fee, a host delivery fee, and a parking charge are separate items. Before the trip, we’ll tell you what to expect based on the booked location.

If your flight is delayed, send us a Turo message as soon as you can. We’ll help coordinate, but if the booked start time needs to change, submit the trip change through Turo. A message alone does not change the official reservation time.

When you find the vehicle, inspect it before driving away. Complete check-in in the app, take clear photos of the outside, inside, fuel or charge level, and odometer, and upload them to Trip photos. If the vehicle seems unsafe, damaged in a way that affects driving, or materially different from what you booked, do not drive it. Contact us and Turo support right away.

## 5. Contactless pickup — calm step-by-step delivery

[Slightly slower, as if guiding someone in real time.]

Contactless pickup is designed to be simple. First, complete every check-in step in the Turo app. Next, confirm the vehicle location from the trip details and our latest Turo message. Then inspect the vehicle and upload your check-in photos before you leave.

Your access instructions may use a secure lockbox or approved remote-unlock method. Never leave keys visible or the vehicle unsecured. If a code does not work, stop and contact us. Do not force a lockbox, window, door handle, or key compartment.

Before driving, check the fuel or battery level, mileage, tire condition, warning lights, and visible exterior condition. Adjust your seat and mirrors. Confirm the correct fuel type for that specific vehicle. If anything does not look right, take a photo and message us before moving the car.

## 6. During the trip — normal questions

[Natural, helpful customer-service tone.]

If you have a question during the trip, message us through Turo so the details stay connected to your reservation. You can also call for quick help, but any official change still needs to be completed in the app.

Only the primary driver and Turo-approved additional drivers may drive. Do not allow an unapproved friend, family member, hotel valet, or coworker to drive the vehicle.

No smoking or vaping is allowed in any Turo vehicle. That includes tobacco, marijuana, electronic cigarettes, and personal vaporizers. Please also check the listing before bringing a pet. Service animals are treated according to applicable law. Non-service pets need a pet-friendly listing or written host permission through Turo, and Turo generally requires them to be transported in a carrier.

The trip’s included mileage appears in the vehicle listing and trip details. If you drive beyond the included amount, Turo may calculate an additional-distance charge after the trip. Unlimited mileage is available only when the listing includes it or offers it as an Extra.

Tolls used during the trip may be billed afterward through Turo. Do not remove, move, cover, or substitute the vehicle’s toll transponder. Tickets, towing, impound charges, and parking violations caused during the trip remain the guest’s responsibility under Turo’s rules.

Return a gas vehicle with the same fuel level it had at pickup and use the fuel grade specified for that vehicle. Return an electric vehicle with the required charge level shown for the trip. Keep receipts and take clear gauge photos. Idle, congestion, and on-trip charging fees may be passed through when they are tied to your trip.

Treat the vehicle as you would want someone to treat yours. No racing, off-road use, towing without authorization, driving under the influence, illegal activity, or any other prohibited use. If you are unsure whether a use is allowed, ask before doing it.

## 7. Extensions, delays, and returns — firm but kind

[Warm, with clear boundaries.]

If you want to keep the vehicle longer, open your booked trip and submit a Change trip request through Turo. An extension is not approved until Turo shows the updated trip details. A phone call, text, or verbal yes from the host does not replace the in-app approval.

Please plan to return the vehicle at the scheduled time. Turo currently provides a thirty-minute return grace period, but you should not rely on it as extra rental time. If you expect to be more than thirty minutes late, request an extension in the app immediately and let us know.

Before returning, follow the latest location and key instructions in the Turo app and our trip messages. Refill the fuel or recharge the battery to the required level. Remove personal items and trash, but ordinary use does not require you to have the vehicle professionally detailed.

At checkout, take clear photos of every side of the vehicle, the interior, odometer, and fuel or charge gauge. Upload them in the app. If you return to a garage or lot, send the section, level, space number, and a photo of the vehicle’s surroundings. Leave the parking ticket where instructed. Secure the key in the approved location and make sure the vehicle is locked.

At F-L-L, use the return location displayed in your trip details, generally Cypress Garage, Level Seven, Zone Seven N-N. At M-I-A, use only one of the currently approved return facilities displayed in the app. Do not return curbside or in an unapproved airport location.

## 8. A worried guest — empathetic range

[Gentle, reassuring, not overly cheerful.]

I understand why that would be worrying. Let’s slow down and handle one thing at a time.

First, are you and everyone with you in a safe location? If there is immediate danger, an injury, smoke, fire, or a collision blocking traffic, call nine-one-one now. Your safety comes before the vehicle.

If everyone is safe, tell me where you are and what happened. Please do not keep driving if the vehicle has a red warning light, a flat tire, fluid leaking, smoke, severe vibration, steering or braking trouble, or damage that could make it unsafe.

Take photos if you can do so safely. Message us through Turo and follow the incident instructions in the app. I’ll stay calm with you, but I will not ask you to take a risk or attempt a repair you are not comfortable making.

## 9. Breakdown and roadside help — urgent but controlled

[Clear, confident, and concise.]

If the vehicle has broken down or will not start, move to a safe location if you can do so without causing more damage. Turn on the hazard lights. Contact us immediately, then call Turo’s twenty-four-seven U.S. roadside assistance line at four-one-five, nine-six-five, four-five-two-five.

Tell roadside assistance your exact location and the known or suspected problem. Stay near the vehicle and keep it in sight if it is safe. Do not arrange your own tow or authorize repairs unless Turo or the host tells you to do so.

If the car is in an unsafe traffic lane, you smell fuel, see smoke, or someone is injured, call nine-one-one first.

## 10. Accident response — serious and compassionate

[Measured and calm.]

I’m sorry this happened. First, are you and your passengers safe?

If anyone may be injured, if traffic is in danger, or if the vehicle is in an unsafe position, call nine-one-one immediately. Do not admit fault or argue with the other driver.

When it is safe, exchange the other driver’s name, address, contact information, vehicle registration, and insurance details. Take clear photos of every vehicle, all visible damage, the road, license plates, and the surrounding area. Collect witness contact information if anyone saw what happened.

Use the Turo incident information card in the vehicle and report the damage through Turo. If the vehicle cannot be driven, contact us and call Turo roadside assistance at four-one-five, nine-six-five, four-five-two-five. If the vehicle can still be driven safely, contact us and submit the damage report within twenty-four hours of the incident.

I cannot decide fault, coverage, reimbursement, or the final claim amount over the phone. Turo and the applicable claims process handle those decisions.

## 11. Frustrated guest — patient and de-escalating

[Let the warmth come down. Sound attentive, not defensive.]

I hear you, and I understand why you’re frustrated.

Let me make sure I have the facts right before I suggest the next step. You expected the vehicle at one location, the instructions showed another, and you have already spent time trying to find it. Is that correct?

Thank you for explaining it. I’m going to focus on what we can do now. I won’t guess, and I won’t promise a refund or fee decision that I’m not authorized to make. I can confirm the trip details, document what happened, help with the immediate pickup issue, and connect you with the right person if this needs a human decision.

If you would rather speak with a person now, I can transfer or arrange a callback. You do not have to repeat the entire story; I’ll summarize the issue for the team.

I can’t give out another guest’s personal information, access code, home address, payment details, or trip history.

## 18. Everyday service story — natural long-form speech

[Tell this like a personal story, not an advertisement.]

The reason we built Rent With Heldy around personal service is simple: most people are not thinking about the rental car when they plan a trip. They are thinking about the flight, the hotel, the children, the cruise, the meeting, the beach, or the family they came to see. The car is only one piece, but if that piece goes wrong, it can make the whole day harder.

We pay attention to the ordinary details because those are the details people remember. Is the car where the message said it would be? Are the instructions clear after a long flight? Is there enough room for the luggage? Does someone answer when the guest is unsure? Can the traveler understand the return plan before the last morning of the trip?

South Florida has its own rhythm. Fort Lauderdale, Hollywood, Aventura, Miami Beach, Brickell, and the cruise ports may look close together, but traffic, airport construction, bridge openings, events, and weather can change a schedule quickly. A calm answer is more useful than a big promise.

Sometimes helping means finding the right vehicle. Sometimes it means telling a guest that the smaller car they chose will not hold six people and six suitcases comfortably. Sometimes it means walking someone through a garage level one step at a time. Sometimes it means saying, honestly, that we need to verify the answer before we give it.

We never want a guest to feel that they are being passed from one anonymous person to another. Even when Turo needs to handle an account, payment, protection, or claims question, we can still explain why the handoff is needed and what the guest should have ready. Good service does not mean pretending we control every outcome. It means taking responsibility for the part we can handle and making the next step clear.

A lot of our work happens before the guest ever reaches the car. We check the schedule, clean and inspect the vehicle, confirm the fuel or charge, review the tires and dashboard, and prepare the pickup details. We think about the handoff from the traveler’s point of view. After a delayed flight, a message that says “Cypress Garage, Level Seven, Zone Seven N-N, space three-eighteen” is much more useful than “the car is at the airport.”

The same is true at return. A guest should know where to park, what photos to take, where to leave the ticket, how to secure the key, and how to confirm the trip is finished. Clear instructions reduce stress for the guest and protect the vehicle for the next trip.

We also believe that boundaries are part of good hospitality. We do not approve an unverified driver because that puts everyone at risk. We do not take a Turo extension off-platform because the protection and trip time need to stay official. We do not guess about a warning light or tell someone to keep driving an unsafe car. We do not promise a refund or claim result before the right person reviews the facts.

Most calls do not need a long speech. They need one calm answer and one helpful question. Where are you now? What time does the flight land? Is everyone safe? Does the app show the change as approved? How many large bags are you bringing? Which garage and space number do you see?

That is the voice we want every guest to hear: local, clear, patient, and practical. Not a call center. Not a sales pitch. Just someone who understands the trip, tells the truth, and helps the guest take the next step.

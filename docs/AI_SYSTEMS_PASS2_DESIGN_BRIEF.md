# AI Systems Showcase — Pass 2 Design Brief

## Design Director read

This remains a hidden B2B case-study landing page for operators, not a product dashboard. Pass 1 already earned a `DISTINCTIVE` verdict through its editorial systems language. Pass 2 should make two existing relationships easier to understand while preserving the page's calm, credible, hospitality-aware character.

The governing principle is **evidence before effect**: interaction must reveal a relationship already present in the data model. The page must remain equally persuasive with transitions removed.

## Skill loadout and dials

- Surface: **marketing / landing / portfolio**
- Sole opinion skill: `taste-skill`
- Motion character: `emil-design-skills`
- Motion entry point: `motion-intelligence` through `motion-director`
- `DESIGN_VARIANCE: 7` — preserve the existing controlled asymmetry; do not introduce a new component language
- `MOTION_INTENSITY: 4` — two restrained explanatory state changes, no ambient choreography
- `VISUAL_DENSITY: 5` — reveal reasoning inside existing structures without turning them into dashboards

## Interaction direction

### Business OS causal trace

- Preserve the six existing stage buttons and their current detail content.
- Give every stage an explicit semantic state: prerequisite, active, or downstream.
- The active stage remains the strongest state. Prerequisites read as a connected completed path; downstream steps stay available but visually quieter.
- Connector state must follow the same causal boundary and remain comprehensible in the mobile vertical layout and RTL.
- Add a short instruction that explains the purpose of selection. Do not add generic progress labels, celebratory completion language, or gamified status.

### Opportunity Finder reasoning assembly

- Keep the fixed local rules and existing recommendation order.
- Under each recommendation, show the exact selected work-pattern labels whose mappings include that recommendation.
- Do not generate prose, confidence scores, percentages, rankings, or simulated analysis.
- Keep the relationship readable as input evidence attached to an output, not as a pile of decorative chips.
- The live region should announce useful output changes without forcing the entire interactive panel into an atomic re-read.

## Brand overrides carried forward

- Retain warm white, sand, ink navy, muted teal, Bricolage Grotesque, and Inter. Inter remains an explicit Rent With Heldy override to taste-skill's default preference.
- Retain one light theme and the existing dedicated showcase tokens.
- Use rules, connectors, typographic hierarchy, and restrained state color. Do not add gradients, glass, glow, generic cards, or badges.
- No generated imagery, stock imagery, or invented performance evidence.
- Existing hero and CTA labels remain unchanged.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Exactly two interactions gain explanatory behavior; no other section receives decorative choreography.
2. Causal and reasoning relationships are legible in text/state with animation disabled.
3. Keyboard and assistive-technology activation is instant; focus indicators never animate.
4. Pointer/touch motion is short, interruptible, compositor-only, and suppressed under reduced motion.
5. All English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, and Hebrew views preserve content, wrapping, touch targets, and directionality.
6. The rendered surface retains an Anti-Slop verdict of **DISTINCTIVE** with no Accessibility, Motion, or Mobile UX blockers.
